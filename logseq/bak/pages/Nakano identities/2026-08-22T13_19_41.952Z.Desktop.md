- Let $\Theta_E:=\nabla_E^2=\partial_E\bar\partial_E+\bar\partial_E\partial_E$ be the curvature operator on $E$-valued forms.
- Write $\Delta_A=AA^*+A^*A$.
- Claim.
	- $[L,\Delta_{\bar\partial_E}]=-i\Theta_E$, $[L,\Delta_{\partial_E}]=i\Theta_E$.
	- $\Delta_{\bar\partial_E}+\Delta_{\partial_E}=\Delta_{\nabla_E}$.
	- $\Delta_{\bar\partial_E}-\Delta_{\partial_E}=[i\Theta_E,\Lambda]$. This is the Bochner-Kodaira-Nakano Identity.
- Proof. Put $A=\partial_E$ and $B=\bar\partial_E$.
	- The [[Kahler identities]] give $[L,B^*]=-iA$, $[L,A^*]=iB$, and $[L,A]=[L,B]=0$. Hence
		- $[L,\Delta_B]=B[L,B^*]+[L,B^*]B=-i(BA+AB)=-i\Theta_E$.
		- $[L,\Delta_A]=A[L,A^*]+[L,A^*]A=i(AB+BA)=i\Theta_E$.
	- We have $A^2=B^2=0$. Also $B^*=-i[\Lambda,A]$ and $A^*=i[\Lambda,B]$, so $AB^*+B^*A=0$ and $BA^*+A^*B=0$. Expanding $\Delta_{A+B}$ gives $\Delta_{\nabla_E}=\Delta_A+\Delta_B$.
	- Write $[-,-]_s$ for the graded commutator. The graded Jacobi identity gives
		- $[i\Theta_E,\Lambda]=i[[A,B]_s,\Lambda]_s$
		- $\phantom{[i\Theta_E,\Lambda]}=i\big([A,[B,\Lambda]_s]_s+[B,[A,\Lambda]_s]_s\big)$
		- $\phantom{[i\Theta_E,\Lambda]}=i\big(i[A,A^*]_s-i[B,B^*]_s\big)=\Delta_B-\Delta_A$. QED
